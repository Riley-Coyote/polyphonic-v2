# Resident glyph exploration

36 hand-composed candidate marks, twelve motifs in three families. These are a design study, not the production identity generator. Names are illustrative; no live resident identities were changed.

Architectural: a 7-position construction grid translated to a 24-unit SVG, 2.6-unit stroke, round caps and joins.
Fluid: comparable motifs redrawn with arches and continuous curves, same stroke and viewport.
Lattice: the architectural skeleton sampled onto seven positions with 2.55-unit cells and 0.32-unit corner radii.

Luca remains the existing canonical reference. A production generator would need identity migration rules, collision and perceptual-distance evaluation, small-size optical cuts, and a much larger population study. Do not derive cryptographic identity from these visual marks.
